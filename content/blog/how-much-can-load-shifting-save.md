# How much can shifting your dryer, dishwasher & washing machine actually save?

Load-shifting — running appliances at cheaper, greener times — sounds like marginal gains. But across three of the most common flexible appliances, the annual savings add up to real money.

## Assumptions

- **Flat standard rate:** 24.5p/kWh peak; same off-peak (no saving possible beyond carbon).
- **Economy 7:** 28p/kWh peak, 15p/kWh off-peak (7 hours overnight).
- **Octopus Agile:** variable; off-peak overnight typically 8–15p/kWh, evening peak 25–50p/kWh.
- Carbon intensity used: peak 280 gCO₂/kWh vs off-peak 110 gCO₂/kWh (illustrative annual averages).
- Frequency: tumble dryer 4×/week, dishwasher 7×/week, washing machine 5×/week.

## Tumble dryer (2.5 kW, 1.5h = 3.75 kWh/cycle)

| Tariff | Cost saving/year | CO₂ saving/year |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Flat rate | £0 | ~104 kg |
| Economy 7 | **£98** | ~104 kg |
| Agile (typical) | **£120–£160** | ~104 kg |

Even on a flat tariff, shifting the dryer to a low-carbon overnight window saves over 100 kg CO₂/year — equivalent to planting 5 trees.

## Dishwasher (1.2 kW, 2h = 2.4 kWh/cycle)

| Tariff | Cost saving/year | CO₂ saving/year |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Flat rate | £0 | ~106 kg |
| Economy 7 | **£68** | ~106 kg |
| Agile (typical) | **£85–£110** | ~106 kg |

Most modern dishwashers have a built-in delay-start timer — making this one of the easiest wins.

## Washing machine (0.8 kW, 1.5h = 1.2 kWh/cycle)

| Tariff | Cost saving/year | CO₂ saving/year |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Flat rate | £0 | ~46 kg |
| Economy 7 | **£22** | ~46 kg |
| Agile (typical) | **£28–£38** | ~46 kg |

## Total household saving (three appliances)

| Tariff | Cost saving/year | CO₂ saving/year |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Economy 7 | **~£188** | **~256 kg** |
| Agile | **~£230–£308** | **~256 kg** |

These are illustrative estimates — your exact saving depends on your tariff terms, appliance wattage, and real-time grid conditions. Use the EnergySaver calculator with your postcode for a personalised figure.

## The verdict

For most households, load-shifting three appliances is worth **£150–£300/year** on a time-of-use tariff, plus roughly **250 kg CO₂** — regardless of tariff. The cost saving is only available to TOU tariff holders, but the carbon saving is available to everyone.
