# Octopus Agile vs Economy 7: which suits a load-shifter?

Economy 7 has been the go-to TOU tariff for UK households for decades. Octopus Agile is its modern, dynamic successor. Both reward flexibility, but they work very differently.

## Economy 7 in brief

Economy 7 gives you **seven hours of cheap electricity overnight** (typically 00:30–07:30 GMT, shifting to 01:30–08:30 BST in summer). The off-peak rate is usually around 15p/kWh vs ~28p/kWh at peak — a saving of ~13p/kWh.

**Good for:** storage heaters, immersion hot water tanks, EV charging on a predictable schedule. Any fixed overnight load that can reliably run every night.

**Not ideal for:** households that can't reliably shift loads into the same overnight window every single day. The window is fixed and inflexible.

## Octopus Agile in brief

Agile prices change **every 30 minutes**, tracking the UK wholesale electricity market. Prices are announced by ~16:00 each day for the following day.

Typical Agile prices:
- **Off-peak overnight (00:00–06:00):** 5–15p/kWh
- **Midday solar surplus (some days):** sometimes 0p or even negative
- **Evening peak (17:00–21:00):** 25–60p/kWh
- **Demand surge events:** can spike to 100p+

**Good for:** flexible households who can shift loads on a daily basis to wherever prices are cheapest — not just overnight but also lunchtime on sunny days. EV owners with smart chargers. Households using smart plugs with automation.

**Not ideal for:** households with rigid routines who can't check prices or set timers daily. Fixed-income households who need bill predictability.

## Head-to-head comparison

| Factor | Economy 7 | Agile |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Rate certainty | High (fixed rates) | Low (changes daily) |
| Best-case off-peak | ~15p | 0–5p (common on windy nights) |
| Window flexibility | Fixed (7h overnight) | Dynamic (any 30-min slot) |
| Carbon alignment | Decent (overnight = lower demand) | Excellent (tracks grid in real time) |
| Smart meter required | Usually yes | Yes |
| Works with EnergySaver | Yes | Yes (live prices) |

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Economy 7 if:** you have storage heaters or a hot water tank, and you can reliably run them every night without thinking about it.

**Choose Agile if:** you have an EV, use smart plugs or home automation, and want to maximise savings by chasing the cheapest half-hourly slots each day.
